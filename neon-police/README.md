## Play it

### ▶ [https://drsamonline.github.io/neon-games/neon-police/](https://drsamonline.github.io/neon-games/neon-police/)

Runs in any modern browser. Once loaded it installs as an offline app and keeps
working with no connection.

**Controls** — `WASD` move · `Shift` run · mouse look · `LMB` fire · `RMB` aim ·
`R` reload · `V` melee · `G` throw · `X` bullet time · `Tab` weapon wheel ·
`1`–`9` weapon slot · `F` interact / drive / **arrest** · `J` disguise · `M` map.
Every key is rebindable in Settings → Controls. Touch controls appear automatically
on a phone or tablet.

Suspects surrender when badly hurt — get close and press `F` to arrest. Arrests
pay better than kills, and evidence builds toward a bigger score.

## What's in it

An open world that generates forever in every direction — twelve biomes, enterable
buildings, drivable cars, grenades and molotovs, a base you build out, a **wanted
system** where the city escalates against you as your heat rises, arrests and
evidence, disguises, boss criminals with weak points, four game modes and a
16-mission campaign.

It is built on the **[Neon Engine](https://github.com/drsamonline/neon-engine)**, where all
the source lives. This repository holds the playable build and the licence.
